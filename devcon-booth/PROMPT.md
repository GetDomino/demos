# The prompt

The exact prompt an unattended coding agent (Claude Code, run headless with nobody answering questions) got for this build. It is the [unattended prompt from the Domino docs](https://console.domino.run/docs/agents#unattended-runs), with the brief and the design written into it. `devcon-photo-booth` is the slug of the Domino project it ran against; use your own project's slug.

````text
Build a Domino campaign from the brief below on a staging site. Nobody is available to answer questions. Run npx -y @domino-sdk/relay-cli@latest checkout devcon-photo-booth, work in the directory it creates, and install dependencies with the command it prints. Then read and follow the Domino skill at node_modules/@domino-sdk/relay-cli/cli/skills/domino/SKILL.md, including its unattended runs section. Stage the result, and end with a report of what you built, the staging link, what you tested, and what you assumed.

Brief: A booth selfie quest for our Devcon booth. Visitors take a selfie at the Domino booth, upload it, and earn 100 points. Start from the unique-photo snippet, so a photo someone else already submitted is caught as reused and goes to staff instead of paying out. The photo must be a selfie or photo taken at a conference booth with a Domino banner or sign visible. Keep the app to one page: the quest, the photo upload, the result, and the visitor's points.

Design: a photo booth. Bold and playful: a sunny page, heavy type, ink outlines with hard shadows, stickers, and a Polaroid that develops after a camera flash. Build it exactly like this:

- Layout: one page in a phone-width column, max 440px, 20px side padding, centered on wider screens. Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono (the starter ships both). Dark mode follows the system setting.
- Colours: sunny page #ffd23f, ink #1b1029 for text, borders and shadows, cream cards #fffdf7, grape #6d4aff, tangerine #ff8a3d, red #e5484d, mint #22c99a, pink #ff6fae. Pink is decoration only.
- Cards have 2px ink borders, 16px corners and a hard 5px ink offset shadow (5px right, 5px down, no blur).
- Header: the Domino wordmark (download https://domino.run/domino-light.svg, https://domino.run/domino-dark.svg for dark mode, and https://domino.run/domino-mark.svg into public/), next to it a pink "Devcon" sticker tilted −6° with an ink border; "Sign out" as an underlined bold link on the right when signed in.
- Headline: Geist weight 800, tight tracking (−0.03em), line height 1. Signed out: "Take a selfie at our booth." at 56px, with a short line under it: "Get the Domino sign in the shot, upload it here, and the points are yours." Signed in: "Take a selfie at the Domino booth" at 34px.
- Signed out: a cream "Sign in to start" card with a pink smiley sticker on its top-right corner, the line "Your points stay with your account.", the email field, and the sign-in buttons.
- Primary button: grape, white text, 56px tall, full width, extra-bold, 2px ink border, 4px ink offset shadow, 14px corners. Pressed, it slides 4px into its shadow. Disabled, it is a pale grape with no shadow.
- Signed in, under the headline, two cards side by side: an ink scoreboard (about two thirds of the width) labelled "POINTS" in small bold caps, with the visitor's points in 44px sunny Geist Mono digits; and a cream status card labelled "STATUS" with one chip. Chips are solid with 2px ink borders and small extra-bold text: grape "Ready", tangerine "Checking", sunny "In review", red "Try again", mint "Completed".
- Below, one cream quest card: one paragraph of instructions ("Find the Domino booth, take a selfie with our banner or sign in the shot, and upload it here. Photos someone else already submitted go to booth staff for a check instead of paying out."), then the upload, then the "Send my selfie" button.
- The upload is a white Polaroid tilted −2°: a thick white frame with a bigger bottom strip, a soft drop shadow, and a mono caption in the bottom strip. Empty, the photo area is a warm grey with a round grape camera button in the middle and the caption "Take or choose a photo"; the whole Polaroid opens the file picker. With a photo chosen, the photo fills the frame and the caption reads "Change".
- Checking: the photo turns grey and blurred and slowly pulses, like a print developing; the caption reads "Checking your photo" with a small spinner; under the Polaroid, "This takes a few seconds."
- Approved: a white camera flash covers the screen and fades out over 650ms; the Polaroid drops in with a spring and settles at −3°; the photo develops from washed-out to full colour over 1.4s; then two stickers slap on: a sunny "+100 points" starburst with an ink border, and a mint "Verified" sticker with an ink border. Caption: "You're in". Under the Polaroid: "Your selfie checks out and the points are in your account." A small mint "+100" pill pops on the scoreboard's top edge during the celebration.
- Sticker placement (important): the caption sits alone on its own line, centred in the bottom strip, and no sticker overlaps it. Both stickers sit in a row of their own just under the caption, centred as a pair with a 12px gap and tilted a few degrees in opposite directions: the starburst on the left, "Verified" on the right. The row is only as tall as the stickers, and the bottom strip grows for it only once they appear. Neither sticker covers any part of the photo, including its corners.
- In review: a sunny "With staff" sticker, tilted, slaps onto the Polaroid's top-right; the caption reads "With booth staff"; under it: "Someone at the booth will look at your photo. This page updates by itself."
- Rejected: a red-bordered "Not quite" card shows the server's reason, followed by "Try another photo.", and an empty Polaroid to try again.
- Footer: a 2px ink rule, then the Domino mark and "Photos are checked by Domino. A photo someone else already sent goes to booth staff instead of earning points."
- Dark mode: page #150d20, cards #231833 with cream borders and grape offset shadows, cream text; the scoreboard turns sunny with ink digits; the Polaroid stays white.
- Motion: the celebration plays once per accepted attempt (remember the attempt in localStorage, so a reload shows the settled state). Under reduced motion, every animation becomes its final still frame.
- Nothing may stick out past the screen edge at any moment, mid-animation included (the flash, the Polaroid's drop and tilt, the stickers, the +100 pill): a phone must never scroll sideways or zoom out. Clip horizontal overflow at the page level.
- Check every state at 390×844 in light and dark before you stage, in mobile emulation (Playwright isMobile: true), and confirm the page's layout width stays 390 from before the upload through the end of the celebration.
````
